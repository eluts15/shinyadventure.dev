About Resumé

👋 Hello, I'm Ethan Luts. I'm passionate about building reliable systems and tooling, and about reducing complexity wherever I find it—which is most of what's kept me in DevOps for the last six years: building and running the infrastructure that lets other engineers ship without thinking about the plumbing underneath them. Multi-cloud IaC, CI/CD pipelines, observability—the details live on the resumé.

Outside of work, I've been learning embedded systems with Rust—pulling firmware down to bare metal, away from the abstractions I spend most of my day working above.

What draws me to it is the boundary itself: the place where software stops being purely logical and has to answer to physics—sensors, timing, voltage, the real world💡
